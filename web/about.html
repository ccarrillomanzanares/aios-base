> What AIOS does

An operating system with its own AI assistant, for ordinary laptops.

The assistant

  • It lives on the laptop: it works without Internet and your conversations stay on it.
  • Talk to it: hold Super+Space and speak; it can answer out loud. Dictate into any window with Super+Ctrl+Space.
  • It runs the system for you: wifi, sound, screens, Bluetooth, brightness, battery, reminders and updates. It asks before changing anything.
  • It can look at your screen (/look) and help with what you copied: summarise it, translate it.
  • Optional: connect a bigger AI model on a server of yours or a provider. Keys are entered on your phone through an encrypted pairing page, and the assistant tells you when data leaves the laptop.

The system

  • Starts straight into a desktop with the WarGames look; the first start asks for language, keyboard, time zone, your password and wifi.
  • Sound for every app, Bluetooth headphones, keyboards and mice, several screens, screen lock and notifications.
  • A firewall that blocks incoming connections; nothing is shared unless you ask.
  • Install it on the disk with your files encrypted, and get signed updates with rollback.
  • aios sys selftest checks the hardware; aios-diag sends us the report if something fails: logs and hardware, never your conversations.

Requirements

  • A 64-bit PC (BIOS or UEFI) with 8 GB of RAM; 16 GB makes the AI about twice as fast.
  • A USB stick of 16 GB or more, or VirtualBox with 8 GB for the virtual machine.
  • On older processors (without AVX2, roughly before 2013-2015) the local AI is very slow: use a server model instead.
  • No graphics card needed: the AI runs on the processor.
